# Service identity assets

These service marks are used only to identify graph destinations. They are not Atlas branding and do not indicate sponsorship or live integrations. All service names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Geometry is preserved; marks are rendered as standalone images.

- OpenAI: https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/openai.svg (MIT collection; copyright 2022 Pablo Hdez). Brand guidance: https://openai.com/brand/
- Grok: the two `id="mark"` paths from the public https://grok.com/ header, with the mark's original coordinate region. Source observed 2026-10-31. Brand guidance: https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines
- Claude, Google Gemini, X, Threads: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons (CC0 collection). Collection licenses do not grant trademark rights.
- Codex terminal icon, Muse/Dots symbol, role and activity icons: original functional icons for this prototype, not official service logos.
