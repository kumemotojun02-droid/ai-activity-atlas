# AI Activity Atlas

Touch-first graph explorer. Open https://kumemotojun02-droid.github.io/ai-activity-atlas/

V3: neural glass styling, orbital light rings, connection pulses and tap feedback. V2: project and AI entry points, semantic depth planes, named agents, activities and shared destinations. All relationships are illustrative samples; assignments and counts are unverified. No external services or private data are connected.


V5 adds two contrasting worlds: a golden idea vault with five illustrative seeds and a bright shopping district with 14 sample store plots. Other projects retain the shared neural map. Idea choices are local to the browser, and do not trigger work.
